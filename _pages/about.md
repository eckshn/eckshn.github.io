Hi, I'm Eckart Schneider, a software engineer at [Optiver](https://optiver.com/) in Chicago.

I graduated from the University of Maryland in December 2025 with a B.S. in Computer Science and a minor in Statistics, which I completed in four semesters. Before Optiver, I interned as a software engineer at Bloomberg, MathWorks and Leidos, worked as a software engineer at the University of Maryland, and was a teaching assistant for CMSC216 (Introduction to Computer Systems). At UMD I was also a senior member of the Smith Investment Fund.

Before college, I interned at the Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory and NextStep Robotics, did hardware and neural network research at George Washington University, and helped lead [FRC Team 4099](https://www.team4099.com/) to 2nd place out of 400+ teams at the 2022 World Championship.

You can find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/eckshn) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/eckshn/), or see my [CV](/cv/).
